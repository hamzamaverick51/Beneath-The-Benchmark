01 / THE QUESTIONPOOL → OPEN WATER

THE ORIGINAL AMBITION

Could a detector trained in controlled water transfer to the real sea?

The goal was to improve detection of underwater objects in a new environment, while understanding how much new-site labelling it would take. That called for more than training runs: the source data, evaluation split, model behaviour and deployment path all needed scrutiny.

RESEARCH SCOPE

Underwater object detection, annotation efficiency, source-model retention and edge inference.

WHAT CHANGED

The benchmark itself turned out to be a research problem. Model scores could not be promoted above the quality of their underlying labels.

03 / EIGHT WEEKS OF WORK

Not one experiment.
A research system.

Five connected workstreams; each produced something durable, and each exposed a limit that informed the next.

01JULY

Reconstruct the baseline

Rebuilt the public-source data path, reconciled dataset counts and class mappings, hardened execution across compute environments, and preserved checkpoints and protocols.

OUTPUT Reproducible preparation path
02JULY–AUGUST

Repair the measuring instrument

Traced augmentation and source-frame lineage, built video-grouped splits, and checked overlap before interpreting cross-domain scores.

OUTPUT Structural split and audit
03AUGUST

Explore model behaviour

Ran bounded studies of label budgets, selection, retention, pseudo-labelling, checkpoint combination and small-object approaches; retained negative and ambiguous results.

OUTPUT 31 historical result records
04LATE AUGUST

Take inference toward the edge

Built NCNN reference and native C++ paths, produced ARM builds, and checked desktop behaviour and emulated-ARM execution—without claiming a board benchmark.

OUTPUT Software prototype and validation
05SEPTEMBER

Make uncertainty reviewable

Screened the full 1,206-frame corpus, assembled a 704-issue candidate register and delivered an offline two-reviewer/adjudicator workflow with validation gates.

OUTPUT Human-review handoff

05 / SOFTWARE TO HARDWARE

From a model file
to an inference path.

The deployment work went beyond a notebook: image, directory, video and camera input paths; an NCNN runtime; a Python-free native C++ path; integrity checks; desktop stability tests; and ARM builds.

The next evidence gate was physical measurement on a specified Raspberry Pi/ROV configuration. That gate was not reached.

06 / THE FINAL HANDOFF

The most useful next step
was human judgment.

Once image orientation and missing/imprecise annotations became material, pushing another model score would have confused measurement with progress. The work shifted to making the data reviewable by the people who could decide what was correct.
